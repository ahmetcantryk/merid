# Card

A content container that groups related information. Cards separate from the page by surface colour rather than a border: a recessed tray on the page, a raised surface on a tray section.


## Examples

### Variants

`tray` (default) is the recessed card for use directly on the page. `outline` is a hairline card for when a tray would clash with its surroundings.

<ComponentPreview code={`<Card variant="tray">…</Card>
<Card variant="outline">…</Card>`}>
  <CardVariants />
</ComponentPreview>

### Elevated, on a tray section

`elevated` is surface plus a soft shadow. Use it when the card sits on a tray [Section](/docs/components/section). A `tray` card inside a tray section flips to this look automatically.

<ComponentPreview code={`<Section tone="tray">
  <Card variant="elevated">…</Card>
</Section>`}>
  <CardElevated />
</ComponentPreview>

### Padding

`none` 0, `sm` 20px, `md` 28px (default), `lg` 32px. Use `none` when the card holds a full-bleed image or table.

<ComponentPreview code={`<Card padding="sm">sm · 20px</Card>
<Card padding="md">md · 28px</Card>
<Card padding="lg">lg · 32px</Card>`}>
  <CardPaddings />
</ComponentPreview>

### Interactive and selected

`interactive` adds a 3px hover lift and a pointer cursor; use it when the whole card is a link or button, and render it as that element with `as`. `selected` draws the 1.5px accent ring.

<ComponentPreview code={`<Card as="a" href="/plans/starter" interactive>…</Card>
<Card as="a" href="/plans/pro" interactive selected aria-current="true">…</Card>`}>
  <CardInteractive />
</ComponentPreview>

## API reference

Renders a `div` unless `as` is given; forwards its ref and passes all HTML attributes through.

## Accessibility

- A card has no role of its own. Give a group of cards a heading, and start each card with a heading at the right level.
- For a clickable card, render it as the interactive element (`as="a"` or `as="button"`) rather than wrapping a `div` in an `onClick`.
- `selected` sets `aria-pressed` only when the card is `interactive` and rendered `as="button"` (a toggle button). Otherwise it is visual only: convey the state with `aria-current` on a link, or a checked control.
