# Elevation

Elevation in Merid comes from surfaces and lines. A card on the page sits on a grey tray, a table is closed by two ink rules, and a shadow appears only when something genuinely floats above the page: a menu, a popover, a toast, a tooltip or a dialog.

## Shadow scale

Shadows are long and faint with a negative spread, so they fall below the surface instead of glowing around it. They are tinted with ink (`rgb(18 20 23 / …)`) rather than black, and every floating surface also has its own 1px hairline. Buttons have no shadow; `--mrd-shadow-accent` is `none`.

<ShadowScale />

`--mrd-shadow-xs` and `--mrd-shadow-sm` resolve to nothing in this identity. They stay in the scale so your own theme has a place to put them back.

## The ink rule

`--mrd-rule` is a 1px line in the ink colour. It closes a table at the top and the bottom and sits under the column headings, the way a ledger is ruled. It is the only dark line in the system; everything else is a `--mrd-line` hairline. In dark mode it flips to the light ink.

## The hairline ring

`--mrd-elevated-ring` is `0 0 0 1px var(--mrd-line)` in both themes. Add it next to the shadow so your own floating surfaces keep an edge even where the shadow is hard to see:

```css
.floating-panel {
  box-shadow: var(--mrd-shadow-lg), var(--mrd-elevated-ring);
}
```

In dark mode the shadows use black at 30–80% opacity.

## Focus

Keyboard focus is a square 2px outline in the accent colour, 2px away from the control. It is built from four tokens, so a product can restyle every focus indicator at once:

```css
outline: var(--mrd-focus-width) var(--mrd-focus-style) var(--mrd-focus-color);
outline-offset: var(--mrd-focus-offset);
```

Fields work differently. They do not get a halo: on focus the border turns accent and `--mrd-focus-ring` (`inset 0 -1px 0` in the accent) thickens the bottom edge to 2px, like a line drawn under the field. An invalid field uses `--mrd-focus-ring-invalid`, the same line in the danger colour.
