# Principles

These eleven rules are the design contract. Every component, doc page and example follows them. If a value is not defined by a token, it is derived from an existing one, never invented.

The rules come from one idea. A meridian is a line on a map, so Merid borrows from map sheets: square-cut frames, ink rules, a legend you can read at a glance, and magenta for the thing that matters.

## 1. One accent, used sparingly

A single accent carries interactivity and selection. The default is map magenta; blue, violet, green, graphite, petrol and brass are available as presets. Everything else is ink-tinted neutrals.

## 2. Surfaces and rules before shadows

Regions separate by tone first, with a grey tray under the page, and by a line second. A shadow means the thing floats above the page, so only menus, popovers, toasts, tooltips and dialogs have one.

## 3. Two line weights

Every border is a 1px `--mrd-line` hairline. The ink rule, `--mrd-rule`, closes a table at the top and the bottom and sits under its column headings. Selection is a 1.5px accent ring around a card, or a 2px accent rule on the leading edge of a row or nav item.

## 4. Shadows only for floating surfaces

Shadows are long and faint and fall below the surface instead of glowing around it. Each floating surface also has its own 1px hairline. Buttons have no shadow.

## 5. Square-cut corners

Controls, menus and tooltips are 2px, cards 3px and dialogs 4px. Full rounding stays only where the round shape means something: the radio, the switch, the slider thumb, step markers and status dots.

## 6. Wide headings, plain body

Display headings use Archivo's wide cut at 600 with negative tracking and balanced wrapping; section titles are slightly wide. Body text is 400 with a 1.6 line height. Weight 700 is never used.

## 7. Three text tones

Ink, body and muted. Hierarchy comes from tone and weight, not colour.

## 8. Quiet interaction

Hover is a background tint. A pressed control sinks by 1px in 80ms; everything else takes 120–160ms on one curve. Reduced motion removes all of it.

## 9. Air

Big section padding, wide gaps, content capped at 1160px and reading width at or below 720px.

## 10. One filled block per page

At most one filled colour block on a page. No gradients; the skeleton shimmer is the only exception.

## 11. No decorative small print

Components never add eyebrows above headings or mono captions for decoration. Table column headings are the only uppercase text, set at a readable 12px, and they follow the page's `lang` so Turkish capitals come out right.
